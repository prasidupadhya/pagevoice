Credits and licenses

Reading, search and document processing run in your browser. Narration is the one exception: the text of each sentence you listen to is sent through this site's narration service to ElevenLabs, which generates the speech. Your books are not uploaded.

Generated speech is produced by a third-party service. Use audio lawfully, and respect the copyright of the book and the terms of the service.
