Credits and licenses

Reading, search, document processing and narration all run in your browser. The voices are fixed model files fetched once, the first time you listen, and checked against pinned hashes. No text from your books is part of any request.

Speech is generated. Voices do not imply the participation or endorsement of a real speaker. Use audio lawfully and respect the copyright of the book.

Exact model revisions, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes are checked into web/src/offline/model-assets.json. Runtime versions are pinned in the npm lockfile and hashes in runtime-assets.json. Voices are fetched the first time you listen, and text recognition the first time you import a scanned PDF.
