Open PageVoice

for PDFs and EPUBs

read, listen to, and understand your documents.

PageVoice is an open-source app for reading and listening to PDFs and EPUBs. Import a file, read it, and listen with a voice that runs on your device. No account is needed.

Open PageVoice View the source

Tested in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Narration is available in English and Spanish.

What PageVoice does

reading

Adjust the theme, text size, and line width to suit you.

  • Three themes, and adjustable text size and line width
  • Contents list, section navigation and bookmarks
  • Find in book ignores accents and highlights every match
  • Focus mode hides everything except the text
  • Remembers your place in each document

listening

Voices run on your device after a one-time download.

  • Kokoro for English and Piper for Spanish
  • The sentence being spoken is highlighted and kept in view
  • Speed from 0.5× to 2×, a sleep timer, and media keys
  • Start listening from any sentence. Audio is prepared ahead.
  • Export prepared chapters as MP3 files. M4B audiobook export is best effort.

understanding

Every result links back to the passage it came from.

  • Keyword search across the whole book, with section citations
  • Detected chapters and front matter you can review and correct
  • Optional meaning search and character-name detection. Both run on your device.
  • No generated summaries or answers. Every result is a passage from the book.

accessibility is the point

Read with your eyes, your ears, or both.

  • Every control works from the keyboard, with visible focus
  • Keyboard shortcuts for play, find, sentence navigation, and focus mode
  • Labeled controls and live status messages for screen readers
  • Text up to 32 px. Three themes. Reduced motion is respected.
  • The spoken sentence is marked by a bar and an icon, not by color alone

local processing, stated plainly

Where documents are processed
Parsing, OCR, speech, and search run in Web Workers inside your browser. PageVoice has no accounts and no server that receives your files.
Where they are stored
In this browser's storage on this device. Clearing site data removes them; library backups are a ZIP file you keep.
What uses the network
Loading the app, and downloading voices or optional models from Hugging Face and jsDelivr when you choose to. Those downloads contain no document text, and the app blocks connections to any other server. No analytics.
One exception
If you pick your device's built-in speech instead of a downloaded voice, your operating system reads the text, and some systems use online services to do it.

supported formats

Formats PageVoice can open
Format What you get
EPUB 2 and 3 Chapters, title, author and cover. DRM-protected books cannot be opened.
PDF with text Sections come from bookmarks and headings. Repeated headers, footers, and page numbers are removed.
Scanned PDF Readable after an optional 25 MB English + Spanish OCR download.

Files up to 100 MB. PageVoice detects each document's language automatically. Narration is available in English and Spanish. The browser needs WebAssembly and IndexedDB. WebGPU is used when the browser has it.

getting started

  1. open PageVoice

    It runs in the browser. You can also install it as an app.

  2. add a PDF or EPUB

    Choose a file or drop it on the page. You can read right away.

  3. download a voice once

    Downloads are about 100 MB, depending on the voice, from Offline tools. After that, listening works offline.
